Sound familiar?

The shot that worked in practice switches off in matches.

  • The serve that was going in now dies in the net.
  • You arrive slightly late and your arm tightens.
  • After two errors you play just to avoid mistakes.
  • At 30-40 your arm brakes and the ball lands short.
  • In the third set you reach the ball one step too late.

The usual answer is to practise more or change something in the swing. That fixes the last link of a chain that started earlier: footwork, timing, distance from the ball, energy left in the tank. I call the source of it your Mother Block: find where the error starts, then train the rest.
